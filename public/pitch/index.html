Appendix · How a requirement is prioritised

Priority score: a transparent weighted average.

Score = Σ (wi · vi) / Σ wi
vi = factor value, 0–100  ·  wi = factor weight
7 factors · default weights sum to 100% · PM can adjust every weight live
Worked example · BMW 1 Series
Reduce friction: High voltage battery
0.25·89 + 0.15·2 + 0.15·54 + 0.15·26
+ 0.10·85 + 0.10·50 + 0.10·65
= 54.6 / 100

AI The AI refines these two judgment factors from the customer study and BMW Group Report, citing its sources. The formula baseline stays visible and every change is logged in the audit trail. All values are clamped to 0–100.
